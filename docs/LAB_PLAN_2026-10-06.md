### Block C, second half — the same ladder with breathing on

Added 6 Oct, after Michael's `a121ded` changed breathing to a **20 s period**
and **±21.4% amplitude** (sides 0.55–0.85 m). Same gain rungs, same mark, same
one real drone — breathing is identical on all three, so it is a constant of
this sub-ladder and `gain_kv` is still the only thing that varies.

At 0.05 Hz, breathing sits 12–30× below the 0.6–1.5 Hz band the oscillation is
measured in, so it cannot contaminate the ripple number.

**One deliberate divergence from Michael's config: `max_accel` 0.5 → 3.5.** At
his new amplitude a 0.5 clamp saturates drone1's commanded acceleration on
**49.5% of ticks** in simulation, peak demand 1.40 m/s². A loop that is
saturated half the time cannot show the linear instability this ladder exists
to find, and all three rungs would read the same because all three would be
clamp-limited rather than gain-limited. At 3.5 the same demand clips 0%.

Measured in simulation at `max_accel: 0.5`, so Michael can see which change did
it — **it is the amplitude, not the period**:

```
30 s, ±10.0%   (before a121ded)   12.6% of ticks clipped
20 s, ±21.4%   (a121ded)          49.5%
20 s, ±10.0%                       7.8%
30 s, ±21.4%                      38.9%
```

That is worth passing on regardless of this ladder: his breathing runs at 0.5
are partly measuring the clamp rather than the gains.

**150 s, not 120.** Breathing starts at 15 s and ramps over 5 s, so 120 s gives
only about 5 cycles; 150 gives 6.5 after the ramp.

## Known traps

- **`crazyflies.yaml` lives in two places.** Editing the repo copy does nothing
  until you `sudo cp` it to `/opt/ros/$ROS_DISTRO/share/crazyflie/config/`. If
  drone_2 will not connect, this is why.
- **drone_2 enabled but unpowered stalls the server.** Disable it for the
  one-drone blocks or leave it powered on the floor.
- **The hexagon config's own launch comment is wrong** — it says
  `hw_drone:=drone1,drone4 cf_name:=drone_1,drone_4`. There is no drone_4
  airframe, and this plan flies the hexagon with ONE real drone anyway. Use
  `hw_drone:=drone1 cf_name:=drone_1 mocap_name:=drone_1`.
- **`hw_drone`, `cf_name` and `mocap_name` are matched positionally.** Getting
  the order wrong drives the wrong agent from the wrong aircraft, which is a
  different failure from the mark swap and looks similar.
- **Firmware setpoint mode persists across restarts.** If a drone behaves
  unlike its siblings, power-cycle it before blaming the config.
- **`takeoff_height:=1.5,0.8`** puts drone1 above drone4. The first value goes
  to the first name in `hw_drone`.
